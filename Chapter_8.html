Information Security and Cyber Law


Concept of Information Security

Information security is the practice of protecting information and information systems from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, destruction, or disruption. It helps keep digital information safe and reliable.


Objectives of Information Security

Confidentiality

Confidentiality ensures that information is accessible only to authorized individuals or systems.


Integrity

Integrity ensures that information remains accurate, complete, and protected from unauthorized modification.


Availability

Availability ensures that authorized users can access information and systems whenever they are required.


Common Cyber Threats

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, disrupt operations, or gain unauthorized access to computers and data.


Phishing

Phishing is a fraudulent technique in which attackers attempt to trick users into revealing sensitive information by pretending to be a trustworthy person or organization.


Password Attacks

Password attacks are attempts to gain unauthorized access to an account by obtaining or guessing its password.


Cyber Law

Cyber law refers to the laws and regulations that govern activities involving computers, digital devices, networks, the Internet, and electronic information. It helps address illegal activities in cyberspace and protects the rights of individuals and organizations.


Importance of Cyber Law

Cyber law helps prevent and address cybercrime, protects personal and organizational information, provides legal recognition to electronic transactions, and promotes responsible use of digital technologies.


Cyber Ethics

Cyber ethics refers to the principles of responsible and appropriate behavior while using computers, digital devices, networks, and the Internet. Users should respect privacy, intellectual property, and the rights of others.


Methods of Protection

Information can be protected by using strong passwords, multi-factor authentication, antivirus software, firewalls, regular software updates, secure backups, and careful handling of suspicious links and messages.
